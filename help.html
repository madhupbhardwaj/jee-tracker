Help & FAQ

How the different parts of the tracker work.

Timers

Only one subject timer runs at a time on a single device — starting a new one automatically pauses the previous one. This is to keep your logged time accurate.

Timers save automatically to your browser, and to the cloud too if you're signed in with Sync.

To-Do List

Tasks are tied to a specific date. Tap a date chip (or the calendar icon for any other date), type a task, and hit +.

Your to-do list is private — no one else in the group can see it, even signed in.

Leaderboard

Shows today's study hours and resets automatically at midnight, based on your device's local time.

All-time is shown as a smaller line under your name — this never resets, and keeps growing across every day you've used the tracker.

Why does "today" sometimes show 0 even though I've studied? The very first time you sync after a leaderboard update goes live, or after any long gap without opening the app, the app sets a fresh "starting point" using whatever time you'd already accumulated. Only time studied after that starting point counts as "today." This only happens once — from then on it tracks correctly and resets cleanly every midnight.

Resetting a subject's timer also resets that subject's contribution to today's leaderboard total, so it won't show a negative or stuck number.

Group Chat

Messages are visible to everyone signed in, and show the date and time they were sent.

Messages are automatically deleted after 48 hours to keep the chat light — nothing is kept long-term.

Sync

Tap the Sync button and sign in with Google to back up your timers and to-dos to the cloud, and to appear on the group Leaderboard and Chat.

Signing in on a second device (like your phone) pulls your latest data automatically — no need to redo anything.
